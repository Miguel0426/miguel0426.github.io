Career I Am Interested In

I am still exploring different careers in information technology, but IT support is one area that interests me. I like the idea of helping people solve computer problems and learning how different systems work. I am also interested in networking because I want to understand more about how computers connect and communicate with each other.

Why IT Support Interest Me

I have done some troubleshooting on my own, and I enjoy the process of finding and fixing problems. For example, I once had an issue with the drivers on my laptop after a Windows update, and I could not figure out what was causing it. I uninstalled and reinstalled the update, then went to the manufacturer's website to reinstall the drivers, but the problem was still there. After that, I checked Device Manager and noticed a warning sign under one of the system devices. I disabled the device, waited a few seconds, and then re-enabled it. That ended up fixing the issue, and I have not had the same problem since. Experiences like this are one reason IT support interests me.

Why Networking Interest me

I find networking interesting because routers and modems are common devices that we see in our homes and almost everywhere we go. I got some basic experience with networking when my parents upgraded their Wi-Fi and we received a new modem from the internet provider. I helped with the basic setup, which made me more interested in learning how networks work.

I would like to spend more time learning about network security, such as using stronger encryption, updating firmware, and understanding how VPNs work. I am also interested in learning about more advanced equipment and systems, including servers and data centers. Networking seems like an area where there is always something to learn, which is one reason I would like to explore it more.
